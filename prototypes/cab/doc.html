Your first variable

3 checkpoints · about 10 minutes

A game has to remember things. How many coins you have. What your name is. Whether a door is open. A variable is how a script remembers something.

step01predict

A box with a name on it

Think of a variable as a box with a label on it:

Read it left to right:

  • local — I am making a new box.
  • coins — the label on the box.
  • = — put this in the box.
  • 10 — the thing going in.

The = does not mean "is equal to" like in maths. It means put the thing on the right into the box on the left.

Now print it. Replace everything in your Script with this:

StudioOutput what you should see
10  -  Server - Script:2

print(coins) looks inside the box and shows what it finds.

quick question predict first

key idea

A variable is a name on a box, and = puts the value on its right into the box.

step03do

Printing more than one thing

Give print several things, separated by commas, and it prints them all on one line with a space between each:

StudioOutput what you should see
Ada has 10 coins  -  Server - Script:4

That line is built from two variables and two bits of fixed text. Change what is in the boxes and the sentence changes with them — that is the whole point of a variable.

step04do

Names matter, exactly

Luau reads names exactly as you typed them. coins, Coins and coin are three different names.

quick question predict first

What will the Output window show? Pick before you press Play.

This is one of the most important things in this whole lesson. A misspelled name does not stop your script. It quietly gives you nil, which is Luau's word for nothing is here.

StudioOutput what you should see
nil  -  Server - Script:2

Studio does try to warn you: look at your script and coin has a wavy underline. Hover over it and Studio says something like Unknown global 'coin'. Get into the habit of checking those underlines — they find mistakes before you even press Play.

Rules for names:

  • letters, numbers and _ only — no spaces
  • cannot start with a number: 2coins is not allowed, coins2 is fine
  • cannot be a word Luau already uses, like local, end or true
  • capital letters count: playerName and PlayerName are different
