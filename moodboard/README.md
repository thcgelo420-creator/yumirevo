# Moodboard

Drop images here when you want a redesign. Claude reads this folder first and
designs from what is in it, not from its own taste.

## How to use it

- **Add images** anywhere in this folder: screenshots, wallpapers, other sites,
  colour palettes, fonts you like. PNG, JPG or GIF.
- **Say what you like about each one** if it is not obvious. Put it in the file
  name (`neon-green-glow.png`, `like-the-tiles-not-the-colours.jpg`) or add a
  line under "Notes" below.
- **Start a new direction** by making a new subfolder (`moodboard/v4-vaporwave/`)
  and saying "redesign from moodboard/v4-vaporwave". Old folders stay as history.
- `current/` holds the images the "night line" design of Oct 2026 was built
  from. The newest folder is the design on screen.

## An earlier design (night line, Oct 2026)

A riced Linux desktop on a late-night train.

- **Colours:** deep indigo glass (`#0c0f26`), one LED green (`#7dffaf`) for
  done/go, one neon pink (`#ff5fc8`) for "you are here", amber for numbers.
- **Type:** Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (reading), IBM Plex Mono (code),
  Pixelify Sans (pixel accents).
- **Shapes:** btop/waybar tiles with the title in the border, a dot-matrix LED
  board on the home page, the course drawn as a train line with stops.
- **Backdrops:** pixel art and night scenes, with optional rain and scanlines.

## Notes

Write what you want here: what to keep, what to drop, what feels wrong.

- District decisions (11 Nov 2026): full neon on workspaces 2 and 3; the
  lesson is the district's big screen with LED columns of real checkpoints in
  the margins; workspace 3 is a cheatsheet wall beside Studio, the terminal
  and files stacked to one side; Ice is Mirror's Edge daylight. The rule that
  keeps it readable: light belongs to signs, prose stays white, each colour
  means one thing, amber is still the lamp.
- Lunar decisions so far: HUD-style top bar (option E, no bar strip) with
  workspaces, streak, clock and date, Night/Ice toggle and settings, all as live
  widgets; the bar changes per workspace; subtle motion; skull mark in the
  style of 03 and 04; wide Y2K titles (Unbounded), LED signs (Doto) and mono for
  code; iridescence everywhere; Night/Ice follows the device setting; ambient
  sound on request. The desk comes first; the other workspaces get redesigned
  later.
